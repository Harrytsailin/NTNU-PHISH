This dataset contains de-identified pre-survey and post-survey responses from 209 participants in Study 2 of a phishing-awareness experiment based on the NTNU Moodle platform. The dataset includes two files: pre_survey.csv, which contains participant background information, security-related behaviors, and the assigned experimental condition, and post_survey.csv, which contains responses collected after the phishing simulation.The two files can be linked using the participant_id variable. Each participant is assigned a unique anonymous identifier ranging from P0001 to P0209, and the same identifier is used in both files. The variant variable in pre_survey.csv indicates the assigned webpage visual condition: A represents the original baseline, B the color-shift condition, C the logo-missing condition, D the icon-missing condition, and E the low-fidelity condition.Researchers may merge the two files using participant_id for statistical analysis.The shared dataset contains only de-identified research data. Direct identifiers, including participant names, email addresses, student IDs, and precise timestamps, have been removed. The anonymous participant identifiers are used only to link records across the two survey files.
